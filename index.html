Which one is for my console? A devkit needs the devkit site — it sets target ID 0x81 and the debugger auth ID, and offers the devkit kstuff build. Retail and testkit both take the ordinary site: a testkit is close enough to retail here that it wants target ID 0x82, not 0x81. Picking the wrong one just fails to jailbreak — it does not harm the console.
